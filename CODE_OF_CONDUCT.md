# Community code of conduct

Welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be patient, respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas while supporting people who are learning.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and disclosure of private information are unacceptable in project spaces and when representing the project.

Use GitHub's private reporting tools to report misconduct. Before community launch, maintainers must add a private reporting contact here. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action: clarification, warnings, content removal, temporary restrictions or permanent exclusion. Maintainers follow the same standards.
